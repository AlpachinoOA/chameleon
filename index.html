Visibility analysis for architects and planners

See what every point of your design can see.

Chameleon measures visibility in 3D models: how much sky, green, water or buildings each point sees, which targets are in view, and what an observer can see. Fast, on your own computer.

What it does

Import your model, give each layer a class, pick your points and run. Chameleon tells you what every spot of your design sees, in minutes, without your model ever leaving your computer.

01

View factor

The share of the view each class fills, from every vantage point, over the full sphere and the upper hemisphere. Sky view factor included.

02

Target visibility

The percentage of chosen targets each spot on your surfaces can see, with weights and distance limits.

03

Viewshed

View cones, domes, corridors and skylines: what an observer sees, painted onto the model.

04

Qualifications

View-out pre-assessments per room with a report, after EN 17037. A pre-assessment, not a certification.

05

Your AI assistant

Connect Claude (Desktop or Code) through MCP: it opens your model, sets up classes and points, runs the studies and explains the results while you watch it work in the app.

06

Yours alone

Everything runs on your computer. Models are never uploaded. Reads OBJ, DAE, 3DM and DXF; results as heatmaps, CSV, JSON and PDF reports.

Consulting

We run the analysis for you

No time or no need to learn the tool? Send us your design and we run the visibility study with Chameleon and deliver a clear report. Packages are by the number of design iterations we analyse, so you can compare options before you decide.

Check

1 design iteration
  • One analysis type of your choice
  • Report with maps, figures and a short reading of the results
  • Delivered as PDF, CSV and the project file

Study

3 to 4 design iterations
  • Compare variants side by side
  • One feedback round between iterations
  • A call to walk through the findings and the design decisions they point to
  • Report per iteration plus a comparison summary

Partner

5+ design iterations
  • An ongoing study through your design process
  • Several analysis types (view factor, targets, viewshed, view-out)
  • Regular calls and a final report

Each package is scoped with you in a short call (model size, number of analyses, deadline). Your model is used only for your study and is not shared, and we are happy to sign an NDA before you send anything. Results are a pre-assessment, not a certification.
