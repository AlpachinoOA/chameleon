Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data we handle when you visit this website, use the Chameleon app or buy a subscription, why, and what rights you have. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

The person or company in the imprint. Contact for privacy questions and requests: atakanalpokan@gmail.com or eda.oezaltay@uni-weimar.de. [Data protection officer, if one is required; otherwise delete this line.]

2. What stays on your computer

The app runs on your computer. Your models, projects, results, pictures, exports and logs are never uploaded to us, and the app has no analytics or tracking. The saved runs kept for each model, your sign-in data for offline use (sealed to your Windows user) and the app's settings live in the app's data folder on your computer. The optional assistant connection also stays on your computer: it listens on the local machine only.

3. What we collect, and why

  • Website visit. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads one script from jsDelivr. Those services see your IP address and browser details when you visit. We do not run analytics or advertising on the website.
  • Free account. Email, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), whether you agreed to emails about Chameleon, when you registered and when you last signed in. Purpose: your account and the free version (contract).
  • Pro subscription and trials. Email, licence key, serial number and end date, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, and for trials the referral code used and the date (one trial per person). Payment details (card, billing name and address, tax data) are collected and kept by Stripe; we never see your card number. Purpose: the contract, licence keys, accounting.
  • Website sign-in. When you ask for a sign-in link, your email is sent to our database provider's sign-in service, which emails you the link.
  • Licence checks. When the app starts, signs in or activates a key, it sends our server your email, the licence key, a random one-time code and a device code (a one-way hash of an ID of your computer, not the ID itself). The server counts computers per licence (at most 2) and frees a seat after 30 days without use or when you sign out. The app also asks the server for the latest version number (this reveals your IP address only).
  • Security and abuse prevention. To limit guessing and misuse we count requests per IP address and per email for a short time (minutes to an hour) in a table that is cleared automatically.
  • When you write to us. Your message, your email address and what you tell us.

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.
